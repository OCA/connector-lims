Integrates **LIMS** with **Odoo Inventory** to track samples as
serialized stock items.

Features
~~~~~~~~

- Adds *Is Specimen* on products; sets the template to storable serial
  tracking
- Links samples to products, lots/serials, and current stock locations
- Auto-creates serial numbers for specimen (or serial-tracked) products
  when missing
- Validates that selected lots belong to the same product
- Shows the current sample location from stock quants
- Smart buttons to open the related lot / serial and its stock move
  lines

Configuration
=============

1. Install **LIMS Stock** (``lims_stock``) after ``lims`` and ``stock``.
2. Open a product (*Inventory > Products*), enable **Is Specimen**.
3. (Optional) Give LIMS users access to stock lots if they need to open
   them.

Usage
=====

1. Create or open a sample (*LIMS > Samples*).
2. Choose a specimen-enabled product.
3. Leave *Lot / Serial* empty to auto-create one from the sample
   identifier.
4. *Current Location* shows the lot's stock location when a quant
   exists.
5. Use the **Lot / Serial** smart button to open the stock lot form.
6. Use **Move Lines** to list stock move lines for that lot.
7. Moving the serial in inventory updates the location when the sample
   is recomputed / reopened.
