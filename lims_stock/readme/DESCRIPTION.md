Integrates **LIMS** with **Odoo Inventory** to track samples as serialized stock
items.

### Features

- Adds *Is Specimen* on products; sets the template to storable serial tracking
- Links samples to products, lots/serials, and current stock locations
- Auto-creates serial numbers for specimen (or serial-tracked) products when missing
- Validates that selected lots belong to the same product
- Shows the current sample location from stock quants
- Smart buttons to open the related lot / serial and its stock move lines
